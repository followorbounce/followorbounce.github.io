Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Last updated 21 October 2026

In short
  • You can use every site here without an account. We don't ask for your name or email, and we never sell or share personal information.
  • We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies. On the pages of the main site we also use Google Analytics, which does set cookies.
  • Preferences like light or dark mode stay in your own browser.
  • Our apps for Threads, Reddit, Bluesky and Mastodon only publish our own posts to our own accounts. They don't collect data about anyone else.

When you visit the websites

Cloudflare Web Analytics (all sites)

Counts page views without cookies or local storage. It records the page, the referring site, the country, and the type of browser and device, and shows them to us only as totals. It does not follow you across other websites. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Google Analytics (main site pages)

The pages at the top level of followorbounce.github.io use Google Analytics 4. The project sites under it do not. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) to tell new visits from returning ones. It collects the pages you view, the referring site, your approximate location (derived from your IP address, which Google does not store), and your device and browser. Google may process this data outside your country, including in the United States. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can block it with your browser's cookie settings, with a content blocker, or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Hosting

The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's servers may log IP addresses to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Tracking parameters in our links

Links in our social posts carry labels such as utm_source=threads. They tell us which network a visit came from, and we only ever see them as totals.

Stored only in your browser

Some pages remember settings in your browser's local storage. Nothing stored there is sent to us. For example:

  • light or dark theme, language, and display options;
  • the knots you marked as learned in the Knot Atlas, and the body you chose in the Anatomy Explorer;
  • instrument and generator settings;
  • the date of birth you enter in Life in weeks, kept so the chart is there next time.

To remove all of it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Microphone. A few audio and visual instruments can use your microphone, but only after you press a button and your browser asks your permission. The sound is processed in your browser and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Our social media accounts and posting apps

We publish short posts, such as a mission of the day or a term of the day, to our own accounts, and some of them are posted automatically. Each automated post is labelled “auto-posted”, or comes from an account marked as an app.

  • Threads: the Meta app herald connects only to our own Threads account. It asks only for permission to identify that account (threads_basic) and to publish to it (threads_content_publish). It does not read your profile, posts, messages or followers, and it receives no information about other Threads users.
  • Reddit: the app followorbounce-daily posts in r/followorbounce and keeps only a list of the items it has already posted, so it never posts the same one twice. It does not collect information about Reddit users.
  • Bluesky and Mastodon: posts are published to our own accounts. The tools do not read other users' data.

The access keys for these accounts are stored encrypted and are used only to publish our posts. When you like, reply to or share a post, that happens on the platform itself, under its own privacy policy.

Data deletion

We don't keep personal data about visitors or social media users, so in most cases there is nothing of yours for us to delete. You can:

  • remove settings stored in your browser by clearing this site's data in your browser settings;
  • remove Google Analytics cookies the same way. If you want analytics data linked to your browser deleted, email us and we will request deletion through Google's tools.

If you connected your own account to one of our apps (they are built for our own accounts, so this should not happen): you can remove the connection on Threads under Settings → Account → Website permissions, or on Reddit under your app permissions. Email followorbounce@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”, and we will confirm within 30 days that any data related to you has been deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email followorbounce@gmail.com. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Children

The sites are educational and open to everyone, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
