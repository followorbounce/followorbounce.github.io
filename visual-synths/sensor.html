Video Synth · Instrument

Sensor

Forty-eight lines lie across the stage like layers of rock. A stream of numbers runs underneath them, and each layer takes it a little later than the one above, so a change ripples down through the strata and leaves a record of itself. You never see the stream itself unless you ask: hover over the stage, or hold it on a touch screen, and the raw signal appears in red along the bottom. Choose what drives it: the tide, a machine's log, your typing, your hand.

How it works

A record, not a graph. The stream is sampled sixty times a second into a ring of 2,048 values kept on the GPU. Across the stage, each line shows the last few seconds of the stream (newest at the right), and line k reads it k × Delay samples later than the top one, with a bend that grows towards the bottom. A spike therefore enters at the right of the top line and steps down through the strata, the way an event is laid down in sediment. The lines are drawn in a fragment shader as one-pixel-smooth distances, so they stay sharp at any size.

The streams. Tide is a harmonic tide model: the four largest constituents, M2 (12.42 h), S2 (12.00 h), K1 (23.93 h) and O1 (25.82 h), with illustrative amplitudes. Pace runs it at hours per second, so you see springs and neaps beat. Machine is a made-up process log: a duty cycle that ramps, holds and drops, with chatter, and now and then a fault spike. Your typing turns every key you press into a pulse that decays. Your hand is the speed of your pointer over the stage. Every stream is scaled by its own running loudness, so quiet and loud streams both bend the strata.

Sound. Eight sine voices on a pentatonic scale, one for each band of strata. Each voice's pitch bends with the stream as its band receives it, so the sound moves downwards with the record. Nothing is sampled; it is the same delayed values that bend the lines.

Lineage

A study in the language of data as hidden driver: a real stream shaping motion without ever being shown, and the image as a stratified record of time. It recreates no specific work.

Limits

  • The tide amplitudes are illustrative, not a prediction for any harbour; the periods are the real ones.
  • The machine log is synthetic. Real sensor archives can be added later as bundled files.
  • Your typing and pointer stay on your device; nothing is recorded or sent.
