Instrument Family · Generative · Interactive

Video Synths

Everything on this site that makes images live — twelve pieces where form, colour and motion are computed every frame instead of drawn. Seven are installation studies: full-frame WebGL works built in the language of museum and festival pieces — point-cloud scans, a real photogrammetry reconstruction, displacement sheets, particle fluids, pure data, a grid of light, a flock of boids. Five are instruments: smaller, knob-driven tools that share one colour engine.

How the family fits together

Every piece is a fully self-contained single .html file. The five instruments share two things instead of rebuilding them: the knob/range-slider control and Palette Synth's cosine-gradient colour formula a + b·cos(2π(c·t + d)), both in /assets/visual-synth-kit.js. The installation studies don't load the kit, but reuse the same formula inside their shaders — Latent Flow colours up to a million particles with it; Point Cloud Scan uses it for depth and thermal colour.

The studies are in conversation with the scan-, data- and light-based installation work of Ryoichi Kurokawa, Refik Anadol, Ryoji Ikeda, NONOTAK, Ouchhh and fuse*. They study a visual language; they are not recreations of specific works and are not affiliated with any of those artists. Each study's page explains its maths, its lineage and its limits.
