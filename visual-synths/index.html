Instrument Family

Visual Synths

Five instruments where wave shape, color, and geometric form are generated live through math instead of drawn by hand — the same spirit as Patch Bay and Signal Chain, but visual instead of (or, in one case, alongside) audio. They share real engines, not just a look: Palette Synth's a + b·cos(2π(c·t+d)) color formula colors three of the other four, and the Additive Wave Sculptor's harmonic sum drives the Lissajous Plotter's two axes.

How the family fits together

Every instrument here is still a fully self-contained single .html file — nothing is imported across pages for markup or behavior — but two things are now shared rather than rebuilt by hand in each one: the knob/range-slider dual control and Palette Synth's cosine-gradient color formula, both in /assets/visual-synth-kit.js (+ a matching .css for the knob styling). That's the one deliberate exception to "every page stands alone" on this site, made only after the duplication was concrete rather than hypothetical — see ROADMAP.md in the repo for the history. The harmonic-sum engine the Wave Sculptor and Lissajous Plotter share is still duplicated between just those two; two copies isn't yet the pattern three or more makes.

Built in order: Palette Synth (color) → Additive Wave Sculptor (wave) → Lissajous Plotter (form, reusing the wave sculptor's engine) → Audio-Reactive Diffusion (wave + form, reusing Media Servers II's Gray-Scott chemistry) → Complex-Plane Fractal Mapper (form, reusing Palette Synth's color engine again). Five instruments, three shared engines, one running idea: a small set of tunable coefficients feeding a trig sum, rendered live.
