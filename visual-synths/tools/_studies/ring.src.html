Ring

A single point of laser light runs round a circle, again and again. The wall behind it is photosensitive: it glows where the beam has just been and fades over seconds, so the circle is always being drawn and always disappearing. Slow it down and you see a comet; speed it up and appearing and fading blur into one ring. Add more rings, let them tilt, and give them sound: low tones hold a ring steady, high tones break it into fragments.

Sound drives breakage
Light

Internal sound plays a low drone with high chimes; the microphone uses the sound of your room. Either starts only when you pick it, and microphone audio never leaves your device.

How it works

A wall that remembers. The wall is a half-float texture read back every frame, multiplied by e^(−dt/τ) where τ is Wall persistence in seconds, and then the beam is added. The beam's head moves a long way between frames at high speed, so the whole arc it swept since the last frame is deposited, not just its end point; that is why fast rings are solid instead of dotted. On top of the wall, the head itself and the beam from the emitter glow in haze: two octaves of drifting noise scale the glow, so the light is uneven, the way it is in a real room with smoke.

Rings in space. Each ring is a circle on its own plane. Tilt in space turns those planes slowly, so a circle becomes an ellipse and opens again, and several rings cross like the orbits of a mechanism. The rings run at related speeds (1, 1.5, 2.25…), so they line up and drift apart over long periods.

Sound into structure. The sound is split into a low band (below 200 Hz) and a high band (above 2 kHz). Low energy pulls each ring's radius towards a steady value; high energy raises Breakage, which gates the deposit into fragments and shakes the radius, so the circle comes apart into dashes and reforms when the sound drops. The internal synth holds a drone and adds high chimes at random moments so you can hear and see the mapping without a microphone.

Lineage

A study in the language of laser light in space: the beam made visible by haze, one continuous path drawn until appearing and fading blur, photosensitive surfaces that keep a trace, and sound turned directly into structure. It recreates no specific work.

Limits

  • The haze is a 2D layer in front of the wall, not a volume; the emitter beam is a straight line in the image.
  • Real lasers scan too fast for the eye at these speeds; here the head is always drawn so you can follow it.
  • Nothing flashes: the head is a moving point and the wall fades smoothly. Reduced motion slows everything to 30 %.
