Silhouette

The flattened outline of a volumetric object, shown without internal depth, detail, or texture.

In practice

A silhouette is a three-dimensional object reduced to its flat outline, filled solid, with no internal detail. It tests whether a shape is recognizable at a glance, which matters for icons, characters and products. A well-designed bottle can be identified from its silhouette alone, and a game character is often refined until its solid black outline is distinct from every other character's.

Often confused with Form

A silhouette is only the flat outline of an object; form includes its full three-dimensional shape and structure.

Try this

Fill the outlines of five household objects with solid black and ask someone to name each one.

Related terms

FormThe perceived shape and structure of an object, independent of its color or surface texture.Counterform (Negative Space)A meaningful shape produced primarily by the empty area around or between positive forms, carrying real visual weight of its own.
