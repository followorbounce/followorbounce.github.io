Dictionary of Inner Experience

Not every word for a feeling names the same kind of thing. This dictionary distinguishes emotions from moods, self-conscious states from existential ones, and a felt experience from a regulation strategy applied to it — and gives each entry a visual built from the same small set of abstract forms, so what changes between two related feelings is genuinely visible, not just described. Rewritten and substantially expanded from an earlier 28-term version at Design Divine Future.

Regulation strategies aren't feelings — they're things a person does with a feeling (suppress it, avoid its trigger, ruminate on it). Kept separate from the main vocabulary above rather than mixed in as if they were another kind of emotion.
